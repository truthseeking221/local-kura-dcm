# Colors

All colour tokens come from `tokens.css`. Switch the **Theme** toolbar between `Light` and `Dark` — the swatches re-key automatically because they read `var(--…)`.

## Brand

The single brand blue used in product chrome.

<Ramp prefix="--brand" stops={[50, 100, 200, 300, 400, 500, 600, 700, 800, 900]} />

## Secondary · Deep (logo navy)

The logo navy lives at `--secondary-deep-500` (`#10069F`). Surrounding stops carry emphasis chrome (Phlebo header, dense data labels). **Never use `-500` itself in product UI** — it's reserved for the lockup.

<Ramp prefix="--secondary-deep" stops={[50, 100, 200, 300, 400, 500, 600, 700, 800, 900]} />

## Secondary · Light (logo cyan)

The logo cyan lives at `--secondary-light-500` (`#60CDFF`). Surrounding stops carry decorative tints (Patient intake illustrations).

<Ramp prefix="--secondary-light" stops={[50, 100, 200, 300, 400, 500, 600, 700, 800, 900]} />

## Ink (neutrals)

`--ink-0` is pure white; `--ink-900` is the darkest text. `--ink-50` and `--ink-150` are anchor stops for surfaces.

<Ramp prefix="--ink" stops={[0, 50, 100, 150, 200, 300, 400, 500, 600, 700, 800, 900]} />

## Status — bg + fg pairs

Always pair a status colour with an icon and a label. Never status-by-colour-alone.
